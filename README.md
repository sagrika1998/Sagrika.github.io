## 🔬 About Me

I am a PhD researcher in **Main-Group Organometallic Chemistry** at the **National Institute of Science Education and Research (NISER), Bhubaneswar**, working in the research group of **Prof. Sharanappa Nembenna**.

My research focuses on the **design, synthesis, structural characterization, and reactivity of main-group and organometallic compounds**, particularly involving **zinc, boron, gallium, and heterobimetallic systems**.

My research experience includes:

- Air- and moisture-sensitive main-group synthesis
- Low-oxidation-state zinc and gallium chemistry
- Bis-guanidinate ligand-supported main-group complexes
- Homogeneous catalysis
- Hydroboration and hydrosilylation
- C–H and C–F bond activation
- Mechanistic investigations using multinuclear NMR spectroscopy
- Single-crystal X-ray crystallography
- Reactivity studies of main-group compounds
- Handling of pyrophoric and air-sensitive reagents using Schlenk-line and glovebox techniques

I am currently seeking **postdoctoral opportunities in main-group and organometallic chemistry**, and I am interested in exploring challenging research problems beyond the specific systems investigated during my PhD.
